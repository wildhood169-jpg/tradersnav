TradingView is the default charting tool for most forex and prop traders — and for good reason. The free plan gives you real-time data, 3 indicators per chart, and a massive library of community-published scripts. For most beginners and intermediate traders, the free tier is more than enough to run a complete strategy.

Where it really shines is the Pine Script ecosystem — thousands of community-built indicators available free. It also integrates directly with several prop firm brokers so you can trade without switching windows.

MRKT AI isn't a replacement for TradingView — it's an AI layer on top. It analyses price structure, sentiment and macro context simultaneously, presenting it cleanly so you don't need to switch between six different sources before each session.

For prop traders, the core value is pre-session preparation. MRKT AI saves 20–30 minutes of research per session — and pre-session clarity directly reduces impulsive trades that blow through drawdown limits. Pair it with ForexFactory to check the red folder news events for the day — together these two cover everything you need before the session opens.

Best used alongside TradingView — use MRKT AI for macro and directional context, then execute on TradingView where your strategy is mapped out.

FX Replay is the most underused tool in a prop trader's stack. Most traders don't backtest at all, or use a broker's clunky built-in replay. FX Replay gives you a dedicated platform to replay real historical price data bar by bar, at any speed, across multiple timeframes simultaneously.

For prop challenges, use this before you start. Replay the last 30 days of your chosen pairs, practise entries and exits, check your stats — without spending a single euro on a challenge fee. Pro tip: cross-reference your replay sessions against ForexFactory's historical calendar to see how your strategy performed around major news events.

TradeZella connects directly to your MT4/MT5 account and auto-imports all your trades — no manual entry. It builds a full analytics dashboard: win rate by session, by pair, by day of week, by setup tag. You can see exactly which setups are profitable and which are bleeding your account.

The standout feature for prop traders is the drawdown tracker — it visualises your daily drawdown against your challenge rules in real time. You see exactly how much buffer remains before your limit. That visual feedback stops the "one more trade" mentality that kills most challenges.

Most prop traders blow their challenges not because they picked the wrong direction — but because they sized their positions incorrectly. One oversized trade in the wrong direction and your daily loss limit is gone. The TradersNav Calculator is built to prevent exactly that.

Before every trade, calculate your exact position size based on your account balance, risk percentage and stop loss distance. The calculator respects FTMO and FundingPips drawdown rules so you never accidentally risk more than your daily limit allows. Takes 10 seconds per trade and can save your entire challenge.

On a $10,000 FTMO account with a 5% daily loss limit, your maximum daily risk is $500. If your stop loss is 20 pips on EURUSD, the calculator tells you exactly how many lots to trade. Same logic applies on FundingPips. No guesswork, no overexposure.
