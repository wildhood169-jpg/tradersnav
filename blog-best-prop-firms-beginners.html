Due Diligence

Red Flags —
Firms to Avoid

The prop firm space has no shortage of firms that look legitimate on the surface but fail when it matters — at payout time. Two patterns in particular should make you walk away immediately.

Red Flag 01
Unjustified Payout Denials
Some firms deny payouts by citing obscure rule violations that weren't clearly stated upfront, or by finding technicalities after the fact. Before joining any firm, search their name on Reddit, Trustpilot, and PropFirmMatch specifically for payout denial complaints — not just general negative reviews. A pattern of this is a dealbreaker regardless of how attractive the challenge terms look.
Red Flag 02
Low Social Presence and Poor Support
A prop firm with no real social media community, no visible founder or team, and a support team that takes days to respond to basic questions is a firm you cannot trust with your money. If they can't answer a simple pre-sale question about their drawdown rules clearly and quickly, they won't handle a payout dispute well either. Community size and support responsiveness are two of the most reliable indicators of a firm's legitimacy — and they're both easy to verify before you spend anything.
Simple Rule

Test their support before you buy. Send a question about their rules. If the response is slow, vague, or unhelpful — that's your answer. Move on.
