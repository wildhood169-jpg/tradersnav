Best Day Rule: No single day can represent more than 50% of your total Positive Days' Profit. If your best day is 62% of total profits, you need to keep trading until it drops below 50%. This isn't a breach — but it means you can't pass on one lucky day alone.

Key Rules You Need to Know

Static vs EOD Trailing Drawdown

The two challenges use different drawdown types — and this is the most important rule difference to understand. The 2-Step uses static drawdown: your 10% floor is fixed at the Initial Simulated Capital and never moves, no matter how much profit you make. On a $10K account, your floor is always $9,000. This is the most forgiving type and the main reason the 2-Step is recommended for most traders.

The 1-Step uses EOD Trailing drawdown: the floor recalculates at end of each trading day based on your highest closing balance. If you grow your $10K account to $11,000 EOD, your new floor becomes $10,000. Profits lock in your floor, which means a reversal after a strong run is more dangerous than on the 2-Step.

News Trading

During the evaluation phase (challenge and verification), news trading is completely unrestricted on both Standard and Swing accounts. Once you're on a funded Standard account, FTMO restricts opening or closing trades within 2 minutes before and after high-impact news releases. The Swing account has no such restrictions at any stage. If news trading is central to your strategy, the Swing account is worth considering.

Weekend & Overnight Holding

Positions can be held over weekends and overnight with no restrictions. Important for swing traders and those trading larger timeframes.

Expert Advisors (EAs)

Private EAs and automated strategies are allowed. Third-party shared EAs used by multiple FTMO traders simultaneously are not permitted — this protects against coordinated exploitation of the firm's books.

Platforms

FTMO supports MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, and cTrader. MT4 support is a notable advantage over newer firms that have dropped it. If your strategy relies on MT4-specific indicators or EAs, FTMO keeps you covered.
