The Real Picture

The "Get Rich Fast" Trap

Most beginners choose crypto for one reason: they've seen someone turn $1,000 into $50,000 and want the same. What they don't see is the thousands who turned $1,000 into $0 trying to do the same thing. Crypto can move 20% in a day — in either direction.

₿ Crypto Reality
High reward, higher risk
Can gain 20% in hours. Can lose 30% in the same session. No closing bell — the market runs 24/7 including weekends.
💱 Forex Reality
Structured, learnable moves
Moves are driven by economic events you can anticipate. Sessions have clear open/close times. Much easier to build consistent habits around.
Volatility

Volatility Is a Double-Edged Sword

Higher volatility means bigger moves — but it also means your stop loss gets hit more often, your risk calculations are harder, and one bad trade can wipe out a week of gains. For a beginner still learning to size positions and manage risk, this is not an advantage.

⚠ The Beginner Trap

Beginners see crypto volatility as opportunity. Experienced traders see it as risk that requires significantly more skill to navigate consistently. High volatility without the skills to manage it is just gambling with extra steps.

Predictability

What Drives Each Market

Forex moves on things you can study, anticipate, and build rules around. Crypto moves on things that are genuinely hard to predict — social media sentiment, whale activity, regulatory rumours. That's not an edge. That's noise.

Trading Hours

24/7 Sounds Good.
It Isn't.

₿ Crypto — 24/7
Always open = always tempting
No structure means no discipline anchor. Beginners end up trading at 3am chasing a move they saw on Twitter. No defined best time to trade.
💱 Forex — Structured Sessions
Clear windows, better setups
London and New York sessions offer the highest volume and most reliable setups. You know when to be at the screen and when to close the laptop.
Prop Trading

Prop Firms Have
Already Voted

If you're serious about prop trading — and most serious traders are, since it lets you trade large capital without risking your own — the market has effectively been chosen for you.

The prop trading industry is built almost entirely on forex and futures. FTMO, FundingPips, Maven — every major firm you'd want to trade with focuses on forex and indices. Crypto prop trading exists but it's a small, less developed space. If getting funded is your goal, forex is the path.

The Practical Reality

Learning forex gives you access to the entire prop trading ecosystem — thousands of funded trader opportunities. Learning crypto prop trading gives you access to a handful of firms with limited instruments and smaller account sizes.

The Answer

Which Should You Start With?

Our Verdict
Start with Forex. Consider Futures Later.

Forex is more predictable, more structured, supported by virtually every prop firm, and has well-established analytical frameworks that beginners can actually learn. The volatility is manageable, the hours are defined, and the learning curve is steep but navigable.

This doesn't mean crypto is permanently off the table. Once you've built consistency in forex, understand risk management deeply, and have a funded account — then exploring crypto as a secondary market is reasonable. But as a starting point for someone still learning to trade? Forex every time.

The Roadmap

Forex first → build consistency → get funded → scale. Crypto can come later if it interests you. Trying to learn crypto first and then pivot to forex prop trading just adds an unnecessary detour to an already steep learning curve.
